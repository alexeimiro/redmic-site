Delete your Redmic account

How to delete your account in Redmic: Voice Keyboard, the app by OKNENOR L.L.C-FZ on Google Play and the App Store, with the data that goes with it. You don't need the app to do it.

Subscribed to Redmic Pro? Deleting your account doesn't cancel the subscription. Cancel it first: on Android in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, and on an iPhone in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Without the app

Write to miralex13@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", from the email address you sign in to Redmic with. If you can't write from that address (for example, you hid your email with Sign in with Apple), write from any address and tell us how you sign in: we'll ask the account's own address to confirm first. We delete the account within 30 days of your request, usually much sooner, and write back when it's done.

In the app

  1. Open Redmic and open the menu.
  2. Tap your account at the bottom, then Delete account, and confirm.

On an iPhone this also revokes Sign in with Apple. Otherwise you can remove Redmic yourself from the apps that use Sign in with Apple, in your Apple Account's settings.

What is deleted

  • Your account: its email address, your name, the account ID, and your sign-ins with Google, Apple or email.
  • Your dictionary, snippets and notes, and the settings saved to the account, such as your writing mode and your data-sharing choice.
  • Your plan, weekly word counts and invite code.
  • The usage and error reports linked to your account, and the notes our staff wrote about it.
  • Your ratings, feedback and support messages saved with the account, and the record of which emails we sent you.
  • The recordings kept for Help improve Redmic, if you turned it on. Turning it off in Settings › Data and privacy deletes them too, without deleting the account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • 30 days: a scrambled fingerprint of the sign-in you used (not your email address or your Apple or Google ID itself), with the words you had dictated that week and whether your free trial and invite month were used. It stops a new account from resetting the free plan: if you sign up again within the 30 days, you carry on where you left off. Then it's erased. Ask us and we'll erase it sooner.
  • About five weeks: the number of free words dictated on each phone that week, under a scrambled form of the phone's id, not tied to any account. It's erased within five weeks of the phone's last dictation on the free plan.
  • While Redmic offers a free trial: on an Android phone, a scrambled form of the phone's id marking that the phone has had its free trial, so the trial stays one per phone. It holds nothing about you. On an iPhone, Apple keeps that mark for us.
  • For our accounts: the records of what your dictations cost us to serve (seconds of audio, amounts of text, which service), without your account. They never held your words.
  • For our sales and tax records: the records of your purchases that Apple or Google sent us (the plan, its price and currency, the store's country and transaction number, and when it renewed, expired or was refunded), and the history of your plan, without your account.
  • Two years: our log of what Redmic's staff did with your account, such as looking at it or giving you Redmic Pro, with the account ID, so that every access to your data stays on record.
  • In our inbox: the support messages you sent us, which reach us by email too. Ask and we'll delete them.

What isn't on our server

  • Your history of transcripts and recordings, and your requests lists, are kept on your phone (apart from the copies of recordings Help improve Redmic sends, while it's on). Uninstall Redmic to delete them; on an iPhone, signing out deletes them too.
  • What you type with the Redmic keyboard never leaves your phone.
  • Google Play and Apple keep their own records of your purchases, under their privacy policies.
  • OpenAI and Anthropic, which write your text, don't train their models on it. Each may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse (OpenAI for up to 30 days), as our Privacy Policy explains.

Deleting some data, not the account

Delete dictionary words, snippets and notes in the app, and they're gone from all your devices. Turn off Usage and error reports in Settings › Data and privacy, and the reports that phone sent are deleted. To have them, or anything else, erased from our server while you keep your account, write to miralex13@gmail.com.
