You always know when it listens.

And what you say stays yours.

  • You can see it.

    While you dictate, Redmic’s mic turns red. On iPhone, iOS shows its orange dot whenever the microphone is open.

  • You start it.

    Nothing is sent until you tap the mic. Lock your iPhone and the microphone turns off within about a minute.

  • You’re asked first.

    Redmic asks before your first recording leaves your phone. The keyboard doesn’t record or send what you type.

  • No ads. No tracking.

    No analytics software. Redmic’s server doesn’t keep your recordings or their text, unless you turn on Help improve Redmic, which is off until you do. Your history of transcripts and recordings stays on your phone.

Speech is turned into text by Soniox or OpenAI, and Anthropic tidies the text. None of them trains its models on your recordings or text.7 Redmic’s server is in the EU, in Frankfurt. You can delete your account any time. Read the Privacy Policy ›

Questions.

How do I set it up?

iPhone. Open Redmic and follow the setup. In Settings › Apps › Redmic › Keyboards, turn on Redmic and Allow Full Access. Then, in any app, touch and hold the globe key and choose Redmic.

Android. Open Redmic and follow the setup. Turn on Redmic in the list of keyboards, then switch to it.

Why does it need Full Access on iPhone?

So the keyboard can pass your dictation to the Redmic app, or send it to Redmic’s server itself. Redmic doesn’t record or send what you type.

Does Redmic open when I tap the mic?

Usually not. The keyboard listens by itself. On iPhone it opens Redmic only when it needs the app, for example right after an update, and then takes you back to your app (from Safari, you swipe back yourself).

Is it really free?

Yes, to start. You get a 14-day trial of Pro, then 1,000 words a week. The four styles, Translate, Dictionary, Snippets and Notes stay free. Pro removes the weekly limit and adds Redmic chat and Redmic AI.

Where do my words go?

Your history of transcripts and recordings stays on your phone. To write your text, a recording goes to a speech service and its text to an AI service. Neither trains on them, and Redmic asks before the first one. If you turn on Help improve Redmic, Redmic’s server also keeps a copy of your recordings, to train Redmic’s own speech recognition, until you turn it off or delete your account. The Privacy Policy has the details.

What if I have no internet?

On iPhone, tap the mic anyway: Redmic opens and writes your words with the phone’s own speech recognition.2 It is a fallback, without the tidying.
