Talk instead of typing.

Redmic is a keyboard for iPhone that writes what you say. Switch to it in any text field, tap the mic and talk. Your words arrive punctuated, with “um” and “uh” gone, and with names spelled the way you spell them.

Download on the App Store

What it does

  • Writes wherever you write. Messages, email, notes, documents and chats with AI.
  • Your words, or your words tidied. Word-to-Word keeps exactly what you said. Rewrite fixes grammar and flow without changing your meaning. Email turns it into a clear, polite email, and Funny makes it playful, with a light joke or emoji.
  • Translates as you speak. Speak one language and type in another.
  • Learns your words. A dictionary for names, brands and jargon, and snippets for what you type often.
  • Keeps your words yours. Your history stays on your iPhone. Redmic asks before a recording goes to the AI services that write your text, and they don’t train on it. Privacy Policy
